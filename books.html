Behind the Stories

My Writing Journey

I have always been told I have a vivid imagination, and I've been making up stories ever since I was a child. My first story, which I wrote at the age of 10, was about me and my family ending up in a strange world. It was simple, and had 5 pages, including the cover... on a font size of 16 (iykyk). But ever since then, I've been writing stories about scenarios that I would like to see. Like me and my secondary school class becoming the heroes of our school by defeating an alien monster (which is something I've written).

At school, I was one of the stranger kids, acting out my own stories in the playground, and I liked reading, which was something that not many of my classmates did. I was always fascinated by the stories I read, and I wanted to create my own worlds, with their own rules and characters. I started writing short stories, and eventually moved on to longer ones, and now I'm working on various series and standalone books.
